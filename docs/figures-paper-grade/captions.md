# 教学样板图注

**Figure 1. Context-dependent experience reuse in an industrial agent (conceptual design).** Process observations form an operating context; candidate experiences are filtered by applicability before relevance-based selection. The planner calls inspection and simulation tools, checks predicted constraints, and either executes an approved action, requests review, or replans. Observed outcomes are verified before updating versioned experience records. E1 is admissible; E2 is expired; E3 lacks required evidence. The waveforms and records are illustrative, not measured results or evidence of safety.

**Figure 2. A three-valued applicability check before relevance ranking (conceptual design).** Each candidate experience e is checked against the current context c for regime compatibility, freshness and constraints. YES indicates supported evidence, NO contradicted evidence, and ? missing evidence. In this example, g(e,c)=1 if and only if every required check is YES. E1 is eligible, E2 is excluded as expired, and E3 remains ineligible until its missing evidence is resolved. Verified updates create a new record version and trigger reevaluation. The fixture illustrates a candidate mechanism and does not establish novelty or effectiveness.
